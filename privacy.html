Privacy

Simply Budgets. Last updated October 2026.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter, including your pay, purchases, balances, goals, and notes, is saved only in this app on your device. There are no accounts and no servers that receive your information.

No tracking, no ads

The app has no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, and loads nothing from other websites. Its fonts and receipt reader are part of the app itself.

Receipts and bank files

When you scan a receipt, the photo is read on your device and is not saved or uploaded. When you check against a bank file, the file is read on your device and only the purchases you choose to add are kept.

No bank logins

Simply Budgets never asks for your bank username or password and never connects to your bank.

Deleting your data

Use Accounts, then Erase everything. Removing the app from your home screen and clearing your browser’s website data for this site also deletes it.
